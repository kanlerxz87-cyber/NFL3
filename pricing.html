PRICING & PRICE STORE

Price snapshots, the price-data policy, and the residual table.

The store records an opening and a closing number for every market this project originates, with the quote’s own time and the capture clock time. The policy decides, before any backtest exists, what price data may be used and what may be published from it. Today the store holds zero rows because zero sources are licensed. That is the correct state, and it is why every result cell on the leaderboard is blank.

Fail-closed by policy. The adopted price-data policy forbids publishing any backtest, ROI, closing-line-value or ranking number until every price row behind it carries a named source, a named licence with permitted use, and both the quote’s own as-of time and the capture clock time. The undated moneyline columns in the community file are not price data under this policy. NFL odds FAQ ↗
