Injuries & lineups

What the official capture establishes about availability, and the difference between a report state, a published depth order and a confirmed starter.

What the capture establishes. The official report establishes a report state for the players it lists, at the capture time. It does not establish that an unlisted player is healthy, that a listed player will start, who replaces them, or how many snaps they will play. Those cells stay blank.
