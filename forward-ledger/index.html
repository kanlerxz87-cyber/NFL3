A point-in-time verified results feed and a pre-registered prospective forecast register, 2026 season

What this workspace is. Two things the rest of the site kept running out of: a point-in-time verified results feed - every 2026 fixture with its official page, its read clock and a cross-check against the league's own standings - and a prospective forecast register for line origination, registered before any result in its window was graded. It is deliberately small: one model, three singles markets, no price, no reward, no ranking until a gate is cleared.
