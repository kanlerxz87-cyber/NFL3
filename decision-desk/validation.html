Validation gates

Ten gates that a strategy must pass before it may appear on a leaderboard. Each gate states the rule, the evidence test and the fail condition, so a rejection can be argued with evidence instead of opinion.

The ten gates.
GateNameRuleEvidence testFails whenMethods source
G01Official provenanceEvery result field traces to an NFL.com or club page opened in this session.Automated check that each data row carries an official URL and that the page returned the value.Unknown host, redirect off the official domain, or a value with no link.NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook 1.3.5.2, confidence limits for the mean
G02As-of integrityNo input that was published after the forecast time may be used.Forecast time, input observation time and kickoff stored per row and compared.Any input timestamp after the forecast time.NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook 1.3.5.2, confidence limits for the mean
G03CompletenessA missing value stays blank and is counted, never filled.Count of blanks per column, with the reason recorded per row.Any imputed value, or a blank presented as zero.NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook 1.3.5.2, confidence limits for the mean
G04Market executabilityA price exists for the exact market at the stated time and venue.A licensed price row must exist for the same market, side and timestamp.No price archive, which blocks every strategy here today.NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook 1.3.5.2, confidence limits for the mean
G05Pre-registrationThe hypothesis, the sample and the acceptance rule are fixed before the test.A dated, hashed configuration and hypothesis record exists before the result.Any parameter adjusted after seeing the result.NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook 1.3.5.2, confidence limits for the mean
G06Out-of-sample skillSkill must beat a naive baseline on data the model never saw.Walk-forward Brier and log loss against coin flip and base rate, with a bootstrap interval.Interval includes zero or the baseline wins.NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook 1.3.5.2, confidence limits for the mean
G07Dependence / combinationsA multi-leg entry is scored on joint outcomes, not the product of legs.Measured leg correlation and joint settlement for the exact combination.Legs multiplied as if independent, or no joint archive.NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook 1.3.5.2, confidence limits for the mean
G08Personnel robustnessThe signal survives losing the players it depends on.Re-score with the personnel actually available, using confirmed participation.No confirmed participation or snap evidence.NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook 1.3.5.2, confidence limits for the mean
G09Prospective shadowA frozen version runs forward for a defined sample before it can be used.Shadow ledger with timestamps, no edits after the fact.No shadow record, or a record edited after kickoff.NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook 1.3.5.2, confidence limits for the mean
G10Reward eligibilityFunding, eligibility and conflict rules are approved before any reward.An approved policy document referenced by the leaderboard.No approved policy; reward column stays blank.NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook 1.3.5.2, confidence limits for the mean

What the gates are not. They are not a backtest and they are not a profitability test. They decide whether a claim is supported, which is the step that has to happen before any claim about money.
