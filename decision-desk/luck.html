Signal, noise and dependence

Luck, skill and correlation

You asked to remove luck and build only selectively skill-based strategies. Luck cannot be removed - but three things can be measured: how much of a rating repeats, how far a record sits from its point differential, and whether skill survives on games the model never saw. All three are below, with intervals.

The key sentence. About 42% of the variance in a nine-week team rating is variance that does not repeat. Any strategy built on this season alone is mostly trading noise, which is why the gates demand out-of-sample and prospective evidence.
