Reproducing the analysis

python3 decision-desk/scripts/crosscheck_games.py   # game log vs official standings
python3 decision-desk/scripts/game_model.py         # ratings, reliability, correlation, walk-forward
python3 decision-desk/scripts/availability.py       # injury to depth join and second-source check
python3 decision-desk/scripts/build.py              # regenerate every page
python3 decision-desk/scripts/check.py               # verify links, row citations and blanks
One honest limitation. In this working environment the shell cannot open a TLS connection to nfl.com, so the official pages were read through a page reader and transcribed into data/games_2026.csv with the exact URL per row. The cross-check against the separately captured standings snapshot is what makes that transcription trustworthy: 154 of 160 team fields reproduce exactly and the six exceptions are the one game the NFL hides.
