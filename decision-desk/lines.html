How a number is produced

The origination protocol, in the order it has to run.
StepWhat is doneWhy it mattersStateSource
1Freeze the official final scores and cross-check them against official standingsA rating built on a mis-transcribed score is worthlessDoneNFL 2026 league standings (regular season)
2Estimate the league mean and sd for totals and for marginsThese are the raw constants a line is made ofDoneNIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook 1.3.5.2, confidence limits for the mean
3Fit opponent-adjusted attack and defence ratings with shrinkageBeating weak opponents must not inflate a ratingDoneNFL 2026 weekly schedules, Weeks 1-9
4Publish an interval, not a pointA number without a band invites false precisionPartialNIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook 1.3.5.2, confidence limits for the mean
