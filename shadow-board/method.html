The same question is registered twice in this repository

A second session registered a comparable evaluation on the same official corpus while this one was being built. The two are not corrections of each other and neither is a replication of the other: they differ in model form, window and sigma treatment, so their numbers must not be pooled or averaged. This table exists so a reader can see which claim comes from which registration. The one thing the two registrations do agree on is the verdict that matters: neither model's skill clears a game-cluster lower bound above zero on the 2026 corpus, so a claim of selective skill from either workspace would be premature regardless of which formula is used.
