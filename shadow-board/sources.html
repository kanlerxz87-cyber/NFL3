SB-F03For Indianapolis at Houston the game centre says RELIANT STADIUM while the schedule page's club travel link says NRG Stadium.venue provenance is contested between two official pages, so the venue field for the other 11 fixtures stays blank rather than being guessed from either.
SB-F04The Cincinnati at Washington fixture shows FedEx Field on the schedule page's travel link and no game-centre read this pass.an unverified venue name would be exactly the kind of inferred value this project forbids; the cell is blank and the check is queued.
SB-F05nfl.com/injuries/league/{season}/REG{week} renders past seasons, but the win-loss records printed beside those tables are end-of-season values.the archive exists and would let the designation-reliability study run, but its point-in-time integrity is unproven; a backtest on it must be labelled with lookahead risk.
SB-F06The Week 10 injury report page titled 'Week 10 of the 2026 Season' prints 'Injuries - WEEK 9' in the body; the same mismatch appears on 2024 Week 4 ('Week 10' title, 'WEEK 4' body).a scraper keyed on the title would mislabel captures by a week; parsers must read the body header.
SB-F07The price-data policy is now written and adopted by a separate register (pricing/PRICE_DATA_POLICY.md), and its fail-closed rule permits nothing yet: zero licensed sources, zero stored price rows, zero residual rows. The undated moneyline columns in the community file are excluded by that policy, which is also this workspace's rule.the market and residual columns here are blank because the adopted policy forbids them, not because no number could be copied in; no strategy can pass Tier 4 while the registers stay empty.
SB-F08This workspace reads the live log at decision-desk/data/games_2026.csv, which currently hashes to the same SHA-256 as the frozen corpus copy, and its gate compares the two every build.a forecast issued against a moving corpus is not reproducible; the pin turns a silent edit into a build failure instead of a quietly different number.
