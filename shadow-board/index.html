Shadow Board - pre-registered singles baseline

One model, five declared entries, no prices, no manual input. The leaderboard shows only what was forecast before kickoff; everything computed from past games is on the backtest page and never beside a ranking.

What this is. A pre-registered singles-only shadow baseline. It prices nothing against a market, because no permissioned price source exists in this repository, so the market and residual columns are blank on purpose. Nothing here is betting advice.
