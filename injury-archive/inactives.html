Official inactive lists

The NFL publishes a weekly article listing each club's inactive players, compiled from the lists clubs must submit before kickoff. It is a different document from the hub at nfl.com/inactives/, and this workspace verified on 2026-11-16 that the hub publishes no 2026 rows while the weekly article does. The article is the source used here.
