What "Questionable" actually means here

The NFL's published rules define the three game-status labels and give no percentage. This page measures the observed inactive rate for each label on joined official documents, with n and a Wilson 95% interval, exactly as frozen in the pre-registration before the first run.

What would change our mind

Would confirm a usable ordinal label: each label's interval separates from the next label's interval, with n ≥ 100 per label, in the pooled cut and in the per-season cuts. Would falsify it: overlapping adjacent intervals, or a pooled ordering that inverts a per-season ordering. In that case the label may not be used as a model feature and the measured rate itself, with its interval, is the only publishable quantity.
