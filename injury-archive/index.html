Official availability, archived before it can be replayed

This workspace captures two official NFL documents — the weekly injury report and the weekly inactives article — with a SHA-256 of the raw bytes and the clock time of every read. It then measures one thing the sources actually support: how often each official game-status designation ends in that week's inactive list. Every number below is computed from committed captures by injury-archive/scripts/build.py.

Why this exists. Two readiness requirements were blocked because the repository held a single day's capture: R6 (archive the weekly reports for past seasons) and A6 (capture the official inactive lists). Both are now scheduled captures with hashes, not a one-off read. A20 and A21 — whether a designation predicts anything, and what "Questionable" empirically means — become measurable only once R6 and A6 exist, so they are reported here as conditional rates with n and an interval, never as a percentage imported from elsewhere.
What this does not prove. Being on the inactive list is not the same as being unable to play, and being active is not the same as playing. This archive holds no snap counts, no starters, no depth order and no replacement assignments. Those need an official participation source, which is still unverified — see Limits and exclusions.
