Validation gates

Every strategy must pass all applicable gates before it can enter a decision leaderboard. The executable gate is tested offline with synthetic fixtures only; those tests are not NFL performance evidence.

Offline tests: 15 unit tests cover capture parsing, blank preservation, odds/vig math, multiplicity, small-sample failure, community-source rejection, future-leak rejection, combo joint evidence and reward withholding.
Ten gates. Unknown or unsupported evidence fails closed.
