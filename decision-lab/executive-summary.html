Executive summary

What is ready, what is blocked

The site is publishable as a research and decision-support dashboard. It is not a validated betting system. The most important improvement is governance: separate official evidence, community exploration, model proposals and approved decisions.

Delivered: clean category dashboard; full requirements/questions/suggestions registers; official injury capture (254 rows); club depth capture (1745 rows); fail-closed validation gates; source-linked flagged-review page; automated capture/build workflow.
Blocked: licensed historical odds/props, official-only outcome settlement, confirmed replacement workload, empirical correlations, validated strategy performance, reward funding/legal approval. These values remain blank.
