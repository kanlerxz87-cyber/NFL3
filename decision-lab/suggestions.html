Suggestions & decision paths

Everything on this page is a proposal. Each option states its dependency, trade-off, success metric and the risk of delay, so a path can be chosen before any change is built. Selections below are optional, browser-local review artifacts: they do not deploy, approve or change any data.

Path A · Foundation firstPath B · Gated expansionPath C · Market-facing product

Paths at a glance

PATH A

Foundation first (recommended)

Fix provenance, parsing and verification; publish the review queue; keep every empirical number blank.

Prerequisites: Official-source contract, automated snapshots, corrected depth parsing.

Cost: Low to medium engineering; no data purchase.

Benefit: Every later number is auditable and the site can be trusted as a decision aid.

Risk: No betting output this season.

PATH B

Gated research expansion

Add singles shadow trials, ablations and proper-score ranking once price and availability evidence exist.

Prerequisites: Path A complete, plus permissioned prices and inactive/snap data.

Cost: Medium to high; data agreement and sustained capture.

Benefit: The first honest skill measurement and the only route to a defensible leaderboard.

Risk: Months of evidence before any claim; may find no exploitable skill.

PATH C

Market-facing product

Publish originated lines, combos and prizes to an audience.

Prerequisites: Path B, legal review, funded rewards, redistribution rights for every data source.

Cost: High; legal, compliance and operational.

Benefit: Audience and reward programme.

Risk: Regulatory exposure, unfunded prize liability, reputational risk from weak evidence.
