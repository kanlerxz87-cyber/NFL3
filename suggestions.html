Recommendation review

Suggestions

A dedicated decision board: compare proposed paths, inspect trade-offs and decide what to pursue before implementation. These are suggestions, not findings of a validated edge.

Review protocol. Each proposal is intentionally separated from implementation. Record a decision (approve, defer, reject), owner, rationale and evidence needed in the eventual project log. This static page does not silently select or execute a proposal.
