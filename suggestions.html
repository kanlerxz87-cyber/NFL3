Decision board

This section makes recommendations explicit and separable from verified facts. The suggested path is to build reliable inputs and validation before trying to win a leaderboard. No reviewer choices have been recorded and no strategy is approved for deployment.

Recommended path: choose Path A first. The critical blocker is the absence of permissioned, timestamped odds and historical line movement—not a lack of clever feature ideas. Developing scores before securing consistent target, price and settlement data risks measuring noise or an untradeable result.
