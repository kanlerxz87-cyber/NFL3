  • Official-page label irregularity: the official Week 1 score page headers the season opener (Patriots at Seahawks, gameday 2026-09-09) as “Sunday Night Football, September 9th”, but 2026-09-09 is a Wednesday — the calendar, the community file (games.csv weekday = Wednesday) and the official schedule pages (as transcribed by official-verify/data/games_2026_official.csv) all agree on Wednesday. The scores-page header appears to be broadcast-package branding, not a weekday fact; flagged for review rather than resolved by inference.
  • Approximate timestamps: each week's observed_at is the approximate wall-clock end of its page read, not a server timestamp.
  • Community file is not official: the feed's values now carry official agreement, but the file itself remains community-maintained nflverse data; re-verification against the official pages is repeatable via results-verification/scripts/crosscheck.py.
  • Week 10 (current week) is not part of the corpus: its games finish 2026-11-12…16 and are excluded until final.
  • Related workspaces (parallel sessions): official-verify froze a schedule-page-derived official corpus (sha256 44f9ea2b…) with a pre-registered walk-forward evaluation (official-verify/prereg/PREREG-2026-OV1.md), and injury-capture runs a scheduled 6-hour injury-report archive.
