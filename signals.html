Hypotheses only · no validated edge

These are testable research ideas, not picks or performance claims. Each must be measured against a predeclared baseline on chronological, held-out data. No actual coefficient, edge, hit rate, ROI, or correlation is available in this repository.

Current line generation is not operational. To originate actionable lines, the system needs licensed historical market prices and a frozen timestamped forecast workflow in addition to official NFL outcomes. Neither has been provided. The values “estimate”, “edge”, and “correlation” below are intentionally blank.

Signal development sequence

01 · defineOne forecast targetSpecify event, market, decision time and settlement.
02 · sourceSnapshot permitted dataPreserve source, time, revisions and missingness.
03 · trainFit simple baselineUse past-only features and keep a final holdout untouched.
04 · compareTest incremental valueMeasure calibration and improvement over benchmark.
05 · publishOnly after gatesShow uncertainty and abstain if weak or stale.

Correlation reporting format

Do not publish a correlation number unless the paired sample, definition, timing, context and uncertainty are also disclosed. For each leg pair, report the data period, sample count, outcome/market definition, marginal rates, dependence statistic, confidence interval, conditional slices, and held-out result.
