RESEARCH

Validation gates

The ten fail-closed gates every strategy must pass, the controls behind them, and the honest limits of separating skill from luck.

Luck cannot be removed. Randomness is not removable from sports outcomes. It can only be controlled statistically: freeze the hypothesis, evaluate on periods never used for fitting, compare against strong baselines, quantify uncertainty and refuse to reward an inconclusive result.
