Evidence before reward

Validation gates

No strategy has passed

The gates below are proposed controls, not completed tests. They are intended to reward repeatable forecast quality rather than short-run wins. Thresholds, sample sizes, markets and benchmarks remain open decisions and must be fixed before evaluation—not retrofitted to a result.

“Remove luck” is not literally possible. Randomness cannot be removed from sports outcomes. It can be controlled for statistically: pre-register strategies, evaluate on unseen periods, quantify uncertainty, compare to strong baselines, and avoid rewards where evidence is inconclusive.

Leaderboard policy proposal

Separate divisions

Keep singles, game parlays, same-game parlays and player-prop combinations in separate tracks. Do not compare strategies with different pricing, settlement or sample availability as if identical.

Every entry needs a frozen model version and prospective forecast timestamp.

Scoring & reward guardrails

Publish probability scores, calibration, benchmark difference, uncertainty, sample size and return only where price / settlement data are valid. Set minimum sample and risk rules before season evaluation.

No reward for an apparent edge whose uncertainty includes no improvement; adjudicate data corrections consistently.
