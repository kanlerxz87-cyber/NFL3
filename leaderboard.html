Competition design

Leaderboard & reward framework

Proposed transparent leaderboard rules for single bets, player props and parlays. There are no entrants or results yet. Rankings must remain unpublished as a skill ranking until the gates below are met.

No one can be ranked from the evidence available. The repo has no archived odds, forecasts, payouts, wager records or validation dataset. “Remove luck” is not literally possible; instead, report uncertainty, use untouched time-based data and demand prospective replication.
