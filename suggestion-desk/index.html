Do we obtain a licensed, timestamped, two-sided price archive - or do we keep publishing originated fair lines with no market comparison at all?

What we already have, verified

FactCommitted artifactSource

0 licensed price sources are registered. The adopted policy bars any price-dependent number until a source carries a named licence, a named source, and both the price's as-of time and the capture clock time.pricing/PRICE_DATA_POLICY.md section 1-2github.com

What is still missing

  • The name of a feed that permits storage, derived models and redistribution - no such provider has been recorded.

Why it comes first. Every downstream number - residual, closing line value, backtest, ROI, rank, reward - is a function of a price we do not have. Ten other decisions cannot be judged until this one is answered, which is why the merged register's own recommendation (SG-02) is to buy the archive before building anything else.

Number that would clear it: A pre-registered with/without comparison on chronological holdouts whose difference interval excludes zero - or, equally useful, one that includes zero and is published as a negative. Today: 0 ablations have been run against a verified pre-kickoff availability set.

Why it comes first. This is the decision the brief is most emphatic about: separate signal from staking, rank with proper scores and uncertainty rather than win rate, and never let a lucky staking run mask a bad signal.

D6 · When may parlays and prop combinations be ranked?

Keep every multi-leg structure behind a joint-evidence gate, or price them by multiplying marginals?

What we already have, verified

FactCommitted artifactSource
The gate exists and is closed: a combination may be ranked only when a directly validated joint probability and the operator's void/push rules are both available. Neither is available.ops-center/data/derived.json SG-15github.com
No leg-level correlation has been estimated from data. The register lists correlation (Q-06, Q-07, Q-08) as unanswered and blocking.ops-center/data/derived.json questionsgithub.com
The singles-only baseline is the registered starting point, and it is the only thing currently allowed to be scored prospectively.shadow-board/, SG-18github.com

What is still missing

  • Any measured joint distribution. Prop lines, participation and settlement at the decision timestamp are all unanswered (Q-56).
  • The operator's void, push and DNP rules, which change the payout of every combination.
  • Whether the dependence between legs is stable across schemes, weather and weeks (Q-08).

Why it comes first. Multiplying marginal probabilities is invalid under dependence, and the register's own proposals say the same thing in four different phrasings. The question is whether to hold the gate or to relax it for coverage.

Governs. SG-13, SG-15, SG-21, SG-41, SG-45 (5 proposals in the register).

Choose a path

Suggested: D6-A. The gate is closed for a reason the register states in four places, and nothing has changed that would justify opening it. D6-B is worth building as research, but it must not be allowed to promote a row.
Number that would clear it: A directly validated joint probability for the legs in question plus the operator's void/push/DNP rules in writing. Today: 0 leg pairs have a measured correlation, and 0 operators have supplied settlement rules.
