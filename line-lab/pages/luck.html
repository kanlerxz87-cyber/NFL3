Luck & regression

A mechanical split between what scoring explains and what it does not — with the residual's limits stated plainly.

What 'luck' means here — and what it does not

Luck is defined narrowly and mechanically: the part of a club's win total that its scoring margin does not explain. It is a residual, and a residual is not automatically luck. It also absorbs special teams, strength of schedule, clutch performance and score effects.

So this page is a list of regression candidates, not a list of bets. Turning a candidate into a bet requires a price to compare against, which does not exist here (F-04), and a market that has not already adjusted, which is an empirical question.
