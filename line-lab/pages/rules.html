Rules

Injury-report and roster rules quoted from official NFL pages, with unconfirmed values left blank and flagged.

These are the rules that turn raw reporting into decision-grade information. Only rules quoted from an official NFL page are marked verified. Where a number could not be confirmed, it was left blank rather than recalled.

Why some obvious numbers are blank

Active-roster size, game-day actives, the game-status publication deadline and the current practice-squad size are all left unverified. They are repeated everywhere, but repetition is not a source. Two of them also conflict: NFL.com reported practice squads of 16, while a 2026 AP article describes 17. Publishing either as fact would breach the rule this project runs on.

Official pages that do not exist

  • An NFL-authored point spread / total / price page — probed https://www.nfl.com/odds/, HTTP 404. No NFL-published odds page exists at that path. Narrower and more accurate statement, confirmed by S13: NFL widgets display odds supplied by partner bookmakers (FanDuel, Caesars, DraftKings) and redirect to the bookmaker. The league does not author, archive or publish lines of its own.

Recording a 404 is deliberate: it stops a future session from citing a URL that looks authoritative and is dead.
