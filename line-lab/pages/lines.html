Model lines

Model-implied spreads and totals for the eight verified Week 10 matchups, with the assumption sensitivity shown rather than hidden.

These are not betting lines

The NFL does not publish its own point spreads or totals: nfl.com/odds/ returns 404 (S09), and NFL Support (S13) confirms that odds on NFL surfaces are supplied by partner bookmakers with bets placed through a redirect. The numbers below are model-implied margins derived from the verified standings. There is no market price in this repository to compare them against, so no claimed edge exists here and none is implied.

Sensitivity: home-field advantage

The home-field term cannot be estimated without a game log, so it is not guessed. Instead every game is published under four values, so the reader can see how much of the conclusion depends on the assumption. The Munich game (New England at Detroit) is flagged neutral and the term is forced to zero.
