F-04 · No NFL-authored odds archive, so nothing can be validated

critical missing information · appears on Lines, Strategy Leaderboard, Validation Gates, Executive Summary
What was found
nfl.com/odds/ returns 404. NFL Support confirms that NFL widgets show odds 'provided by official bookmaker partners of the NFL (FanDuel, Caesars, and DraftKings)' and that bets are placed through a redirect to the bookmaker. So odds are visible on NFL surfaces, but they are a partner feed, not an NFL product, and no historical archive is published. Consequently no closing line, CLV, ATS record, backtest or leaderboard score exists anywhere in this project.
Why it matters
Lines are supplied by licensed sportsbooks, not authored by the league. This is a structural fact about the source landscape, not a limitation of effort. Anything extracted from a partner widget would be a snapshot of a third party's price, with no guarantee of history or continuity.
How it is handled
Every model output is labelled a model-implied number, never a line. The leaderboard publishes zero scored strategies rather than fabricated ones.
To clear it
Purchase a historical odds archive (SG-01) and define the closing-line snapshot rule in writing (Q-04).

F-08 · Depth order (1st/2nd/3rd string) is not in the official roster feed

critical missing information · appears on Depth & Availability, Strategy Leaderboard
What was found
The official NFL.com roster page exposes player, number, position, roster status, height, weight, experience and college. It has no depth-rank column. It was read in full to confirm this.
Why it matters
The central requirement — detecting when a 2nd, 3rd or 4th string player must replace a starter — is a depth-order question. Without an order, any 'backup' label would be an invention.
How it is handled
The DepthStress index measures verified availability degradation by position group. It explicitly does not claim who replaces whom, and says so in its own definition.
To clear it
Q-09 / SG-05: verify per-club official depth charts, or derive order from an official snap-count source, for at least 30 of 32 clubs.

F-02 · Core roster numbers could not be confirmed on an official page

high unverified values · appears on Rules, Depth & Availability
What was found
Active-roster size, game-day active size, the game-status publication deadline, and the current practice-squad size are all left blank or marked unverified. An Associated Press article surfaced in search describing a 17-man practice squad, which conflicts with the NFL.com report of 16.
Why it matters
These numbers set the frame for every availability calculation. Publishing a remembered number as a verified one would be exactly the failure mode this project forbids.
How it is handled
Rules R05, R09, R11 and R12 are marked unverified. R09 carries the conflict note.
To clear it
Find an official 2026 NFL Football Operations roster-rules page (or the current CBA summary) and quote the four figures directly.

F-09 · No leg-level outcomes, so no correlation coefficient is estimated

high missing information · appears on Strategy Leaderboard, Validation Gates
What was found
The only correlations computed in this project are cross-sectional team correlations (C1-C8) from season aggregates. No pairwise leg correlation for parlays or props exists.
Why it matters
Parlay and combo pricing depends entirely on the joint distribution. Any rho used would be an assumption dressed as a measurement.
How it is handled
The copula mathematics is implemented and published; every parlay strategy is BLOCKED at G9. The illustrative tables state their parameters explicitly and are marked synthetic.
To clear it
Q-14: build a leg-level outcome table for at least two seasons and estimate rho per market cluster.

F-03 · Most game statuses did not exist yet at fetch time, and two clubs were not captured

medium gap · appears on Injuries, Depth & Availability
What was found
Of 108 transcribed injury rows, only 6 carried a game status (Out / Questionable). The remaining 102 were practice-report entries only. Buffalo's report was captured partially and the New York Jets report was not captured at all, because ingestion stopped part-way through the source page.
Why it matters
For a Sunday game fetched on a Thursday this is the expected state under the league's own scheme, but it means this week's extract cannot support a start/sit decision on its own. The BUF/NYJ omission is an ingestion limit, not a data property.
How it is handled
Coverage is stated on the Injuries page and in the dataset metadata. No status was inferred from a practice line.
To clear it
Re-snapshot the same page on Friday and Saturday; complete the BUF and NYJ reports.

F-10 · 'Questionable' has no measured calibration

medium unverified value · appears on Depth & Availability, Injuries
What was found
The definition is verified (uncertain whether the player will play). The actual play rate of players carrying the designation is not measured, and this week's extract has only 6 designated players.
Why it matters
If a designation does not predict participation, every model feature built on it is noise. Assuming a probability would be an invented value.
How it is handled
No probability is assigned to any designation. The availability weights used in DepthStress are declared assumptions (A-04), not calibrations.
To clear it
Q-20 / SG-06: join several seasons of designations to official inactive lists and compute play rates with confidence intervals.

F-01 · Clubs have played an unequal number of games

low data irregularity · appears on Standings, Lines, Luck & Regression
What was found
In the 2026 standings snapshot, games played range from 8 to 9 across the 32 clubs because of bye weeks and the in-progress Week 10 slate.
Why it matters
Per-game rates are still comparable, but raw season totals (PF, PA, wins) are not. Any ranking that mixes totals across clubs with different game counts is wrong.
How it is handled
Ratings are computed per game. The games-played column is published so the user can see the mismatch.
To clear it
None needed; it is a real property of a mid-season snapshot.

F-05 · Week 10 slate is only half captured

low gap · appears on Lines
What was found
Eight of the sixteen Week 10 games were verified with their dates, kickoff times and networks. The remainder were beyond the truncated portion of the source pages.
Why it matters
The model lines therefore cover half a slate. No matchup was invented to fill the gap.
How it is handled
The 8 verified games are published; coverage is stated as 8 of 16.
To clear it
Fetch the remaining schedule pages or use a full-slate feed.
