Questions & gaps

Every question that must be answered before a decision, what we know, what we do not, and the cheapest way to close each one.

How to use this register

Every row is a question that must be answered before a decision in its area is more than a guess. The Blocks column names the decision that cannot proceed; the last column names the cheapest action that closes the gap. A row marked blocked cannot be closed by research — it needs money, a legal opinion, or a human choice.

Status definitions: answered = closed against an official source or reproducible arithmetic · partial = some evidence, not enough · unknown = nothing gathered · blocked = needs an external decision.
