Strategy leaderboard

Twelve strategies across single bets, parlays and player props — with every gate state shown, including the ones that block them.

Zero of twelve strategies are scored — and that is the honest answer

The board ranks nothing because nothing can be measured. Every strategy is blocked at gate G0: the league publishes no odds archive of its own (S09, S13) and none has been purchased, so there is no price, no closing line and no way to separate an edge from a coin flip. Parlay and prop entries are additionally blocked at G9 and G10 for missing leg-level and usage data.

Publishing an empty board is a deliberate choice. Filling it with hypothetical results would be precisely the failure this project exists to prevent.

How the board will rank once G0 clears

composite = 0.40·z(mean CLV) + 0.30·z(shrunk ROI) + 0.20·stability + 0.10·capacity shrunk_ROI = ROI × n / (n + 400)

The shrinkage term is the important one: with 400 graded bets, half the measured ROI is treated as signal and half as noise. A 30-bet heater cannot lead the board, by construction.

Grading spec: price fixed at submission, pushes refunded, voids excluded, and a line move never re-grades a placed bet. See Validation gates for the 13 gates and the reward tiers.

ST-01 — Rating-mismatch spread single bet

spread / moneyline
Thesis
Bet when the model's margin disagrees with the market by more than a declared threshold, and the disagreement is not explained by information the market has and the model does not (injuries, weather, rest).
Rule
If |model_margin - market_margin| >= 2.0 points after an injury adjustment, take the side the model prefers at the best available price.
Verdict
BLOCKED at G0 — no odds archive (F-04). The model exists; the market does not.
How it fails
The model currently lacks opponent adjustment, so most 'disagreements' with a sharp market would be model error, not edge.

ST-02 — Pythagorean luck regression single bet

spread / win total
Thesis
Clubs whose win total runs ahead of their scoring margin should regress. This build measures that gap directly: 76.7% of win-pct variance is explained by scoring margin, leaving 23.3% as the pool where luck lives.
Rule
Fade clubs with luck_wins >= +1.5, back clubs with luck_wins <= -1.5, only while the market has not already priced the regression.
Current examples
Denver +2.01 and Cleveland +1.99 expected wins above margin; Tampa Bay -2.48 and Houston -1.59 below. See the Luck page.
Verdict
BLOCKED at G0. The signal is computed from official data; the test is impossible without prices.
How it fails
The 'luck' residual also contains real information the margin misses (special teams, clutch play, opponent quality). Treating all of it as luck would be an error, and this is the main way this strategy can be wrong.

ST-03 — Rest and schedule asymmetry single bet

spread
Thesis
Unequal rest (Thursday games, bye weeks, international travel) is a priced-in discount that is sometimes mispriced. This season has nine international games and a Week 11 schedule change, so the population of unusual spots is unusually large.
Rule
Flag games with rest differential >= 3 days or a travel-time zone change >= 5 hours; require a model edge beyond the declared threshold.
Verdict
BLOCKED at G0. Schedule facts are verifiable; the effect size is not estimable here.
How it fails
Small effects, heavily studied by the market. The prior should be that any edge is already gone.

ST-04 — Divisional compression single bet

spread / total
Thesis
Familiarity compresses margins. The measured correlation between division form and overall form is only +0.33 (p=0.066, n=32), i.e. division results carry real but modest extra information.
Rule
In divisional games, shade the underdog and the under. Reject if the market total already sits below the model total by the declared threshold.
Verdict
BLOCKED at G0.
How it fails
C6 is a cross-sectional correlation, not an effect estimate on margins. Using it as an effect size would be a category error.

ST-05 — Two-leg same-game parlay (team total + spread) parlay

SGP
Thesis
Two legs on the same team are strongly positively correlated, so an independent-leg price overpays the bettor. The engine quantifies the size of that mispricing for any rho.
Rule
Price the joint with a Gaussian copula and estimated rho; bet only if the offered SGP price exceeds the copula fair price by the declared margin.
Verdict
BLOCKED at G0 and G9 — no SGP prices and no leg-level dataset to estimate rho (F-09).
How it fails
Books apply their own correlation adjustment. If their rho is better estimated than ours, the 'edge' is our error.

ST-06 — Two-leg cross-game parlay parlay

parlay
Thesis
Legs in different games are close to independent, so the product rule is roughly valid. The book's parlay price is therefore the fair product minus a hold — a pure price-taking bet with no informational edge.
Rule
Never bet without a leg-level edge; the parlay multiplier cannot create one.
Verdict
BLOCKED at G0. Included on the board deliberately as the control case: if this strategy ever shows profit, suspect the grading, not the skill.
How it fails
Compounding the book's margin across legs. Structurally -EV unless each leg carries a real edge.

ST-07 — Quarterback-out offence fade single bet

spread / team total
Thesis
A verified starting-QB absence is the largest single availability shock in the sport. The injury report makes the absence official; the market has to reprice it.
Rule
If the starting QB is Out or Doubtful on the official report, apply the declared QB weight to the team's model rating and require a residual edge.
Current examples
Week 10 verified designations include Kyler Murray (MIN, did not participate, no game status yet) and TreVeyon Henderson (NE, Out, illness).
Verdict
BLOCKED at G0, and additionally dependent on Q-09 for the 'starting QB' determination.
How it fails
Without a verified depth order, 'the starter' is an assumption. That is why Q-09 is a P0 question.

ST-08 — Secondary attrition versus the pass single bet

team total / pass props
Thesis
Cornerback and safety attrition is the defensive analogue of a QB absence, and it is visible on the official report by position.
Rule
Count verified CB/S unavailability, weight by the declared defensive-back weights, and compare the repriced total with the market.
Current examples
Verified Week 10 examples: Carlton Davis III (NE, CB, knee, Out); Malik Harrison (NYG, LB, groin, Questionable); D.J. Reed (DET, CB, did not participate, no status yet).
Verdict
BLOCKED at G0. The availability half is verified; the valuation half is an explicit assumption (A-04).
How it fails
Position weights are the weak link. Publish both a low and a high weight set and require the signal to survive both (SG-15).

ST-09 — Running-back workload prop player prop

rushing yards / attempts
Thesis
Opportunity drives production. A back whose snap share and carry share are verified rising is mispriced if the market lags usage.
Rule
Project attempts from recent share, convert to yards with a per-carry distribution, and price the over. Blocked unless the player is confirmed active.
Verdict
BLOCKED at G0, G10 — no usage data (Q-03) and no confirmation route for participation.
How it fails
Usage models overfit to the last two games. Needs the full season's distribution, not a hot stretch.

ST-11 — Three-leg same-game script parlay parlay

SGP
Thesis
QB passing yards + WR receiving yards + team total over share a common driver. The equicorrelated factor model shows the joint probability is materially higher than the product of the legs.
Rule
Price with the three-leg copula; require the offered price to exceed the copula fair price by the declared margin; reject if rho cannot be estimated.
Verdict
BLOCKED at G0, G9, G10. Highest variance of anything here; the most gate exposure; the most attractive to overconfident operators. That combination is why G9/G10 are hard gates.
How it fails
Highest model risk on the board. Even a correct rho can be wrong enough to flip the sign of the EV.
