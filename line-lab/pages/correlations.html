Reading these honestly

  • C1 is the skill signal. It is high because margin and wins share the same scoring events — that is the point, not an artefact. It sets the ceiling on how much of a record is explainable at all.
  • C4 is the interesting one. Offence and defence are close to independent across clubs (r = −0.206, p = 0.26), so neither alone predicts the other. That is why a single net-points rating carries more information than two separate rankings.
  • C7 is flagged mechanical. Last-five form is a subset of the season record, so the correlation is inflated by construction. It is shown to make the size of that artefact visible, not as evidence.
  • C8 is reliability, not momentum. Two disjoint blocks of the same season correlate at +0.424, but both blocks measure the same club quality. Calling that momentum would be a category error.
  • What is missing. No leg-level correlation exists, so nothing here prices a parlay (F-09). These are cross-sectional team correlations, which are a different object entirely.
