What the rules say about these columns

  • Practice: exactly one of Did Not Participate, Limited Participation (<100% of normal reps), Full Participation (100%). Source S03.
  • Game status: Out = will not play; Doubtful = unlikely; Questionable = uncertain. 'Probable' was eliminated because ~95% of players carrying it played. Source S03.
  • Blank status: means no designation was published at fetch time. It does not mean the player is healthy, and it does not mean he will play.
