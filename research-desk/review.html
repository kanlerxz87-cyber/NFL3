Irregularities found during the review

  • The 2025 and later official depth charts no longer carry depth_team; they use pos_slot and pos_rank. The 1st/2nd/3rd-string field exists only for 2021-2024, so any multi-season backup study must run on that window.
  • The official schedule's spread_line sign convention is positive = home favoured, the opposite of the common sportsbook display. The engine converts it in exactly one place so a sign error cannot silently flip every graded bet.
  • Team codes differ between sources for a few franchises (WSH/WAS, JAC/JAX, LA/LAR). One normalisation table is applied before any join.
  • report_status is sometimes blank while practice_status is populated. The availability measure therefore uses game status only and treats a blank as "not ruled out", never as "healthy".
  • The repository started from a single empty README; nothing pre-existing was verifiable, which is why every number on this site is generated rather than quoted.

Cross-check against the NFL's own pages

The official nfl.com pages are the public face of the same feeds. They are used to confirm field semantics and to spot-check values, not as a bulk source: a live page cannot be cited as permanent evidence for a historical number.
