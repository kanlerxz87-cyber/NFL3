Suggestion board

The list to review and choose from before implementing anything. Each row states the evidence, the cost, the test that would prove it wrong, and the validation gate it must pass.

How to choose. Prefer suggestions that are cheap, falsifiable and gated. A suggestion that cannot fail a test is an opinion, not an experiment.
Implemented = already shipped in this build; Recommended next = highest evidence per unit of cost. This table records proposals and their evidence status, so its rows carry a detail-page link rather than a data value.
