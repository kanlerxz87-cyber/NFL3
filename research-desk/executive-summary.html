Executive summary

The state of the work in one page: what has been verified, what the numbers say, what fails, and what must happen before any change is implemented.

Data verified. 16 official release asset(s) matched their GitHub-published SHA-256 digest; newest official asset timestamp 2026-11-12T07:46:47Z. See the verification register →

1 · What was built

  • A verified data layer. Official nflverse release assets are downloaded by CI and each file is checked against the SHA-256 digest GitHub publishes for that asset. A digest mismatch stops the build.
  • An original-line engine. A walk-forward model fits only on prior seasons, so every out-of-sample prediction was made before the games it grades.
  • A validation regime. Seven pre-registered gates, with multiple-testing and selection-adjustment controls, applied to a fixed family of strategies.
  • An availability layer. Official weekly injury reports joined to the same week's official depth chart, so a starter listed Out is linked to the backup who replaced him.

4 · Correlation

Across 2,926 games, home win + market-favourite cover + over all landed together 14.6% of the time versus 12.8% if the three legs were independent — a lift of 1.14x. Same-game legs must be priced with a joint probability, not a product.

5 · What is still unknown

The desk cannot yet price player props or parlay combinations on real joint outcomes, because no verified, licensed player-prop history or player-prop closing line is in the snapshot. That gap is recorded as a question with a named owner and the exact data needed, not estimated. See Questions &amp; gaps and Flagged for review .
