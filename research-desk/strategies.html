Reward design

Rewards are tied to gate completion, never to raw profit, so a lucky streak earns nothing:

Proposal - requires a decision on the suggestion board before any reward is paid
Reward tierRequirementWhy this cannot be gamed by luck
CandidateG1 sample size metA small sample cannot pass, so a hot start earns nothing.
QualifiedG2 profit CI above zeroThe interval must exclude zero, not just the point estimate.
VerifiedG3 + G4 multiple-testing and selection gatesCorrects for how many strategies were tried; a lucky winner from a large search does not qualify.
DurableG5 stability + G6 closing-line valueRequires repeatability across seasons and halves, and beating the closing number.
DeployableG7 slippage stressMust survive a 10% worse price, so thin edges are filtered out.
ChampionAll seven, sustained next seasonOut-of-sample survival in a season it never saw.
