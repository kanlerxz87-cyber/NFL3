MODEL vs MARKET

Line origination: our number versus the market

Every game gets an independent spread and total from a transparent model. The model is refitted each season on earlier seasons only, so the numbers below are genuinely out of sample.

Definition of our spread: positive means the home team is favoured, matching the release's schedule schema. Model inputs: pre-game Elo, prior-six-game scoring-margin form, rest differential and a home-field term. Totals: prior-six-game points for and against on both sides of the ball, blended with the league's scoring level.

Grading the disagreements at the market's own price

A bet is only taken when our number is more than 0.5 points away from the closing line. Profit is per unit staked, graded at the market's actual price for that side, pushes excluded from the win rate but kept at zero profit.
