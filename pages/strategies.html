MODEL NOTES / PROPOSED

Price the joint outcome, not the story.

For a single event with estimated probability p, a fair decimal price is 1/p (excluding pushes and costs). For a parlay, use the estimated joint probability, not the product of marginal probabilities unless independence is justified. Operator settlement rules and available prices must be checked first.

For pairs of binary outcomes, estimate Pearson/phi correlation from aligned observations, report sample size and game-clustered confidence intervals, and check out of sample. Correlation depends on market definitions and context; it is not a joint probability. No numeric correlations can be verified yet.
