Availability, depth and the limits of the official sources

Which starters are out is answerable from the official report. Who replaces them, and with what workload, is not answerable from any source obtained here — so it is blank, not estimated.

Nothing in this table is inferred, which is why it is short. The league's depth-chart route and its snap-count route both returned the league's own 404 page on the capture date. Rather than reconstruct a depth order from roster status, jersey numbers, salary or experience — all of which the brief explicitly rejects — the columns that would need those inputs are left blank and the requirements that depend on them are marked blocked.
