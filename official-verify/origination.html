Originated lines: side, then total, then moneyline

Thirteen Week 11 fixtures priced from the verified corpus. Our number, the market number and the residual live in separate columns; the market column is blank because no price source exists here.

Order of origination, frozen: side first, then the total, then the moneyline. The side and the total are the two numbers a market actually quotes; the moneyline is a monotone transform of the side once a distribution is assumed, so it is originated last rather than invented independently. The order is recorded on every row.
Why the market column is blank. No official or licensed price series is held by this repository, and the official pages fetched here publish results rather than prices. The residual is therefore blank rather than zero, because "we could not compare" is not the same claim as "we agree with the market". Filling this column with a community or scraped number would make the whole table unverifiable. The policy is published in the source register and flagged as OV-F05.

Every number below is the output of the pre-registered fit, re-fitted on all 137 verified games. The probability uses a fixed margin standard deviation of 13.5; that value was fixed in the registration and has not been tuned. Each row carries the parameters used, so a reviewer can recompute it by hand.
