How to check a number yourself

Every table row has an official link. The corpus check is one command and it compares our game log against official standings arithmetic field by field:

python3 official-verify/scripts/verify_corpus.py

To re-derive every score on the site:

python3 official-verify/scripts/evaluate.py
python3 official-verify/scripts/analysis.py
python3 official-verify/scripts/originate.py

What this workspace is for

You asked for an NFL line-origination and strategy system with no manual input, every number traceable to an official source, and no invented values. This is that, built the way the evidence allowed:

  • A verified results corpus. Every 2026 game through Week 10 was re-read from the official weekly schedule pages, then cross-checked against official standings arithmetic. The check caught a real transcription error, which was corrected at the source.
  • A pre-registration that predates its results. The rule text, the corpus hashes, the model form, the window, the controls and the gates were committed before any score existed. A gate fails the build if that is ever untrue.
  • Originated lines in the order you asked for. Side, then total, then moneyline, with our number, the market number and the residual as separate columns. The market column is blank, because no price source exists here, and it says so.
  • A realism layer. Luck removed from the win table, calibration published including the bins too small to read, and controls on every board.
  • An honest refusal list. Depth, snap counts, inactive lists and dated injury series were tested and could not be obtained. Rather than approximate them, the requirements that need them are marked blocked, with the exact number that would clear them.
