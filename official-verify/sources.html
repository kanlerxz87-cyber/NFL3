What could not be obtained

These are published because a missing input is a finding. Each one is the reason a requirement is blocked rather than approximated.

  • OV-B01 · Official market prices (closing lines, moneylines, totals) for 2026 games — not obtained. NFL.com pages fetched here publish results and schedules, not historical prices. No licensed price archive is available to this session. The origination board therefore publishes our own number with the market column blank and flag OV-F02. No backtest ROI, closing-line value or vig decomposition is published.
  • OV-B02 · Official depth-chart ordering by club — not obtained. The nfl.com depth-chart route 404s (OV-S14). Club depth pages are owned by other sessions' captures and were not re-fetched by this session. No depth rank, starter/backup label or replacement order is asserted in this workspace.
  • OV-B03 · Official snap counts and inactive lists — not obtained. The tested league snap-count route 404s (OV-S15). Snap share and inactive lists are therefore absent; the workspace does not infer them.
  • OV-B04 · Point-in-time (dated) official availability series — not obtained. The injury report page is a current-state page. One capture cannot be replayed, so no availability feature is used in any model published here.

The price policy, decided before any backtest

Decision: no market price is published until a licensed or official price series with a stated closing definition is obtained. The consequence is that every market column stays blank and every residual stays blank. A blank is never filled with a community or scraped number, and it is never rendered as zero disagreement. The number that would clear it: two vendor evaluations in writing, one reuse grant, and a fixed closing-snapshot definition.

Source authority boundary: this workspace cites official NFL pages for published facts. A citation supports only the field it is attached to, and it does not imply reuse rights. Community-maintained data is deliberately excluded from every number on this site.
