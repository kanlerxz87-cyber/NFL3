Suggestions, ranked by value and cost

Twenty proposed improvements, each two-sided, each with its dependency and its cost. Nothing on this page is implemented until you choose it.

How to use this page. Nothing here has been implemented without your say-so. Each suggestion states the problem, the smallest change that addresses it, its cost and risk, what it depends on, and — two-sided, as you asked — the observation that would prove it wrong. Pick the ones you want and they move to the requirements page.
The order that is cheapest first. S01, S07, S09 and S11 cost almost nothing and change how the site is trusted rather than what it computes. S02 (a second season) is the single highest-value item on the list, because it is the only one that can move the leaderboard's failing interval.
