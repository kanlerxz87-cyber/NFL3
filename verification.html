05 / LIMITS

What this verification does not prove

Stated so it is not over-read.

NOT PROVEN

Historical point-in-time truth

The verification compares the capture with today's live page. It cannot turn a mutable page into an archive, and it does not establish what was known before any earlier kickoff.

NOT PROVEN

Reuse permission or prediction

Opening an official page proves what it publishes. It does not grant reuse rights, and a faithful copy of a report still carries no predictive claim.

RE-RUN

The verification runs on every deployment; on a networked runner it re-captures the official pages and checks the whole population.
