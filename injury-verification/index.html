LIMITS

What this does not prove

One fetch, one day: this is not a link crawler, not a historical archive, and not proof the page will stay unchanged. Field values were never inferred — where the page is blank, the row is blank. Game-status columns fill in as game time approaches; timing effects are flagged below, not filled in. Related verification workspaces cover other angles: verification-lab/ re-checks published claims claim by claim, and decision-lab/verification.html logs capture-time checks; this page adds the three-way extractor comparison and its captured evidence file.
