THE RESEARCH DESK

A clearer view of the game

A decision-first workspace for NFL data, independent lines, and disciplined strategy evaluation. Every table row on this site carries a link to the page it came from. Anything that cannot be confirmed from an official source is left blank and flagged.

Start with the ops centre. It is the consolidated view: who is injured and who the club publishes behind them, what we still do not know, what to do about it, and why no strategy has been scored yet. Nothing here is betting advice, a sportsbook, or an approved wagering system.
