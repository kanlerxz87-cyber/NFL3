01 / THE BRIEF

What is established, and what is not

Verified facts and open items are kept apart on purpose.

ESTABLISHED

The official capture is faithful and reproducible.

254 injury rows and 1,745 club depth rows were captured from official NFL and club pages with a document hash. 151 injury rows and 60 club depth rows were then re-read line by line from the live pages: every row that also existed in the capture matched exactly, and the 43 that had changed are listed on the verification report.

Read the verification report →
NOT ESTABLISHED

No price, no edge, no reward.

No licensed historical odds or player-prop feed is established, so no independent line can be compared with a market price, no leg correlation can be measured and no leaderboard entry can be scored. The gates exist; nothing has passed them. The capture itself is a point in time: 43 of the rows read had already changed since it was taken.
