Executive summary · 12 November 2026

NFL Decision Lab

A transparent research and review workspace for building NFL market signals, tracking lineup availability, and testing strategy skill. The repository began empty; this is an evidence-led blueprint, not a live odds feed or a claim of predictive performance.

Executive readout

Decision-ready foundation; no validated edge yet.

Official NFL pages were checked as source routes for schedules, player/team statistics, team directories, injury reports and rules. No player/game values were copied into a dataset. Lines, prop prices, depth-chart replacement quality, return history, market baselines and backtest outcomes are not established here. Until those evidence gaps are closed, no strategy should be ranked as profitable or rewarded as skilled.
