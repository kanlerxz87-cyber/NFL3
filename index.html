01 / THE BRIEF

Executive summary

A clear starting point for the work ahead.

THE OBJECTIVE

Build conviction, not just predictions.

Develop independent NFL prices and compare candidate strategies using reproducible, time-aware evidence. Start with singles; evaluate parlays and player-prop combinations only when joint outcomes and settlement rules are available.

Explore the strategy lab →
THE REALITY

Evidence comes before execution.

This is a research and planning dashboard. No strategy is approved, no correlations have been measured, and hourly snapshot refresh is scheduled, but it is not a real-time alerting service. Luck cannot be eliminated; uncertainty can only be quantified and reduced.
