Overview

Official reserve-list and trade captures joined to the published club depth rows, so that a player who is unavailable can never be read as available because a report did not list them.

Why this exists. The weekly injury report only lists players who are on it. A player who has been placed on the reserve list is unavailable and may not appear on the report at all, so "not listed" must never be read as "available". This ledger joins the official reserve list and trade log to the published club depth rows so that the gap is visible instead of silent.

The three findings

Stale depth rows

9 published club depth rows name a player who had already been placed on the reserve list before the chart was captured. A depth chart that still shows a player who is unavailable will make the next man look like a starter.

Traded-away rows

0 published depth rows name a player the club traded away before the chart was captured. Depth that is not versioned against the roster log becomes a fiction within days.

Report coverage

40 of 50 reserve-listed players do not appear on the captured weekly report at all. Their unavailability is established by the reserve list, not by the report.
