Executive Summary: Architecture, Verified Facts & Go/No-Go Blockers

An end-to-end briefing for stakeholders reviewing what official sources establish today, why single-source injury tracking and naive parlay multiplication fail, and what must be unlocked before live line origination.

Core Conclusion 01

Decision Readiness Precedes Line Execution

While official NFL rules, club 1st–4th string depth charts, Next Gen Stats definitions, and Nevada regulatory hold benchmarks are verified today, zero live originated lines or strategy edges are claimed until licensed historical odds, play-by-play archives, and jurisdictional approvals close Questions Q-01 through Q-12.

Core Conclusion 02

Why Multi-Source Depth Tracking Is Mandatory

Cross-checking 2026 Week 9 & Week 10 nfl.com/injuries/ reports against 7 official NFL club depth charts proved that single-source trackers fail: IR stars (e.g., Lamar Jackson) drop off weekly injury tables while remaining 1st-string on club depth charts, and injured rotational players (Lucas Patrick, Malik Harrison, Kerby Joseph) can be omitted from club two-deeps.

Core Conclusion 03

Controlling Variance vs. Skill in Parlays & Combos

Statewide Nevada Gaming Control Board filings confirm Parlay Cards hold 27.79% (Sep 2025) / 8.31% (12-month) versus 6.70% / 5.05% on straight Football pools. Consequently, leaderboard rewards must rank strategies strictly by post-gate out-of-sample Brier Skill Score and vig-free Closing Line Value—never raw short-term ROI.
