05 · Origination, Signals, Gates, Correlations & Rewards

NFL Line Origination, Validation Gates & Skill Leaderboard

Research framework for originating independent NFL lines and evaluating single bets, parlays, and player prop combos through 8 validation gates designed to reward repeatable forecasting skill over luck.

Selective Skill vs. Variance Note: Aleatoric in-game variance (fumble bounces, tipped interceptions, overtime coin tosses) cannot be deleted from single NFL contests, but luck in strategy ranking and reward allocation is systematically stripped out by enforcing Gates G1–G8, vig-free Closing Line Value (CLV), Murphy Brier decomposition, multiple-testing FDR haircuts, and 8-week prospective shadow lockouts. All empirical score, correlation (rho), and rank cells remain strictly blank ("") until licensed historical play-by-play and closing-odds feeds are connected.
Fair Line Origination
Marginal Fair Decimal Price O_fair = (1 - p_push) / p_win Explicitly models push mass on NFL key numbers (3, 7, 10) before comparing against vig-stripped market consensus.
2-Leg Correlated Parlay / Combo
Exact Binary Joint Probability P(A & B) = P(A)P(B) + rho_AB * sqrt(P(A)(1-P(A))P(B)(1-P(B))) Originate parlay/combo only if P(A & B) at the lower 95% CI bound of rho_AB exceeds implied bookmaker SGP probability.
Skill Separation Gate
Brier Skill Score & Cluster t-Stat BSS = 1 - (BS_model / BS_market_vigfree), t = mean(d_g) / (sd(d_g)/sqrt(G)) Clustered by independent game G so correlated same-game props never inflate sample size N_eff.
