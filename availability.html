Lineup intelligence specification

Injuries, roles & replacements

Track availability separately from playing role. The NFL injury report provides an official route for reported player injuries, practice participation and game status. This repository has no verified team-week injury/depth records, and no replacement impact values are assumed.

How a useful replacement record should work: preserve what was known at each pregame cutoff, link the player to a team/position/role at that time, record the source and effective time, then measure actual offensive or defensive participation. “Second-string” is not inferred from season statistics or a current roster alone.

Suggested processing flow

  1. Capture reports as-of time. Store raw source, source URL, team/game, retrieval timestamp and report revision; distinguish report date from collection time. NFL report source.
  2. Join official identity and club. Record player identifier, team, listed position and effective date; retain unresolved joins as unverified. NFL player directory.
  3. Attach team-chart provenance. Save the club's depth-chart source and exact publication date; rank replacements only where an official ordered chart is explicit. NFL official team sites index.
  4. Measure actual role and outcome. Join participation/usage and game stats using a verified provider; do not equate a listed backup with a known skill penalty. NFL player statistics.
  5. Estimate incremental signal out of time. Compare lineup-adjusted forecast to a frozen baseline, report uncertainty and keep the coefficient blank unless empirically estimated. NFL team statistics.

No medical diagnosis is inferred. Only reported status and authorized team-source facts should be stored. All current player/game-specific values remain blank.
