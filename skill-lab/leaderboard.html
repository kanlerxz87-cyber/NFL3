DivisionStatusWhySource
1 · Singles (spread, total, moneyline)Admissible6 rows scored with proper scores; 0 passed the gates.protocol §11
2 · Game parlaysNot admissibleNo permissioned parlay price history and no operator void/push rule set exists in this repository, so no directly validated joint probability can be produced.NFL odds terms
3 · Same-game parlaysNot admissibleThe legs of one game are measurably dependent (see Dependence), so multiplying marginal probabilities is invalid; no joint probability can be validated without the operator rules.2026 rulebook
4 · Player-prop combinationsNot admissibleNo official prop line history and no official settlement record exists in this repository.committed artifact
Non-admissible divisions are published as empty with the reason. They are not omitted and they are not scored with a number that cannot be validated.
