Dependence between the legs of one game

Dependence

Measured joint probabilities against the independence product: the evidence that keeps the parlay divisions empty.

Why the parlay divisions are empty. A parlay price is a joint claim. Multiplying marginal probabilities is only valid under independence, and the legs of one game are not independent: the observed joint probability of home-covering and over is 0.1769 against 0.2187 if they were — a gap of 0.0418. A combo may only be ranked when a directly validated joint probability and the operator’s void and push rules are both available. Neither exists in this repository.
