Methodology and limitations

Methodology

The frozen protocol, its commit hash, every definition and every limitation that travels with the numbers.

The rule was frozen before the results were computed. The protocol is committed in the repository; the creating commit is d80b8453bf6148c74cfaf6b6388fa1ec8498fa21. The amendment that corrected the wording of the random control was committed before any score existed and is published with it. Any later change needs a new numbered pre-registration.

What is measured

  • Corpus — 2026 regular season, weeks 1–9: every game with both scores published. Observed 136 games against an expected 136.
  • Model — walk-forward: coefficients are fitted on earlier seasons only and applied to the target season. Two margin models (Elo + form + rest, and Elo only) and two total models (least squares and a shrinkage estimator) are published, so the totals are never collapsed into one number before the walk-forward comparison.
  • Probability — a normal distribution around the model’s own line, using the training residual scale carried forward unchanged.
  • Benchmark — the de-vigged closing price probability, primary method proportional, secondary method Shin.
  • Proper scores — Brier score and log loss, with skill defined as 1 − (model score ÷ benchmark score).
  • Uncertainty — a cluster bootstrap that resamples games, so the markets of one game are never treated as independent evidence.
  • Multiplicity — Benjamini–Hochberg at q = 0.05 across the family, with the trial count published.

Limitations published with every number

  • Feature selection is not blinded. The walk-forward refit removes coefficient lookahead but cannot remove a human having chosen the feature list with access to historical results.
  • The prices are community-maintained. The closing lines come from the nflverse schedules release. They are not an official NFL price archive, and no price on this site is published as official.
  • No dated price history. With one undated line per game there is no opening price, so closing-line value is blank and the gate that depends on it cannot be evaluated.
  • One season of hold-out. The primary corpus is a single season. The supporting panel over earlier seasons is published beside it, not merged into it.
  • Result scores come from a community release; the standings cross-check on the Decision Desk page is what ties the corpus back to the official record.
