What the run found

No registered strategy passed the gates. On the frozen 136-game corpus, every registered row scored worse than the market’s own de-vigged closing probability on its market: the best row was spread_full at -0.0294 Brier skill, with a 95% game-cluster interval of [-0.0961, +0.0345]. The point estimate is below zero and the interval spans it, so the honest status is no skill demonstrated — this is not a strategy that is nearly right, it is a strategy that did not beat the market’s own probability. Any staking result from this family would be luck, not edge.

Signal, not staking

A strategy is ranked on how good its probability is against the market's own probability. A profitable staking run cannot promote a row that fails the signal gates.

Luck is measured, not asserted

Every run publishes a random-pick control and a base-rate control. A row that cannot beat the 95th percentile of the random control has not shown skill.

Uncertainty is structural

Intervals come from a bootstrap that resamples games, so the three markets of one game are never counted as independent evidence.

Multiplicity is counted

Benjamini–Hochberg control at q = 0.05 is applied across the whole family, and the number of trials is published with every adjusted value.

Blank means blank

There is no dated price history in this repository, so closing-line value, open-to-close movement and any official price column are blank and flagged — never estimated.

Official sources only for facts

Results, injury reports, reserve-list moves and standings come from NFL.com. The closing prices are community-maintained and are labelled as such on every table.

How to read this

  • Leaderboard — the six registered rows with their Brier and log-loss scores, the game-cluster interval, the multiplicity-adjusted value and every gate outcome.
  • Controls & luck — the market benchmark, the random-pick control, the base-rate control and the favourites-only control, with the luck floor each row had to clear.
  • Staking view — the retrospective return at the published closing price, kept in a separate table because it cannot promote a row.
  • Dependence — the measured dependence between the markets of the same game, which is why marginal probabilities are not multiplied for parlays.
  • Methodology — the frozen protocol, its commit hash, every definition and the limitations that are published with the numbers.

Within-game dependence, measured on the same corpus

Home covers and over occurred together in 23 of 130 graded games (0.1769), against 0.2187 if the two legs were independent.
