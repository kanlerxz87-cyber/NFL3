Why there is no closing-line value column

Closing-line value needs an opening price and a closing price for the same market at a known time. This repository holds one undated community line per game and no official or permissioned price history, so the cell stays blank rather than being estimated. Source boundary: NFL odds-widget terms (support.nfl.com).

What would fill the blanks

  • A permissioned, dated open and close price history for every market we originate.
  • An official settlement record so a graded result is not a community score.
  • A published price-data policy, which the readiness register lists as blocked.
