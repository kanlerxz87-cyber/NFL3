Questions & requirements

Open questions · no assumptions

This is the minimum discovery checklist before choosing a data vendor, defining a model or publishing a betting signal. “Confirmed” means the linked official NFL page was inspected for the cited kind of information; it does not certify complete historical availability, a licensing right, or a prediction.

How to read this page: known source capabilities are separated from information not present in this repository. Empty/unknown fields must not be filled using guesses. All proposed answers are questions to resolve, not decisions already made.
