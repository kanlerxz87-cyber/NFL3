Every model total, twice

One corpus, two total estimators, a gap column and a pre-registered collapse rule. Scored against official final totals only.

Not collapsed by rule. Both estimators stay published with the gap column, which is what requirement P4 asks for until one wins walk-forward. Four of the five pre-registered conditions fail on the validation block; the numbers that fail them are printed below, not summarised away.

It publishes no price, no ROI and no staking number, because the price firewall in pricing/PRICE_DATA_POLICY.md is still closed at zero licensed sources.

What the gap column exposed

Why T2 loses, measured rather than argued. Bias is the mean signed error (prediction minus truth); a bias-corrected MAE subtracts that constant error from every prediction, which is a diagnostic only — it is not a new published estimator.
EstimatorBias SD of errorSD of prediction Corr(prediction, truth)MAE Bias-corrected MAE (diagnostic)Source
T1-0.1114.141.690.172411.4611.46official Week 9 schedule page ↗
T2-7.4614.310.670.055012.6711.59official Week 9 schedule page ↗

T2 prices 7.46 points below the realised total on average, while T1's bias is -0.11. Subtracting that constant only takes T2 from MAE 12.67 to 11.59, so the bias is not the whole story: T2's prediction spread is 0.67 points against a realised spread of 14.33, and its correlation with the realised total is 0.055 against 0.172 for T1. An estimator that varies by under a point cannot track a target that varies by fourteen. The cause is mechanical and is stated in the flagged table rather than repaired here: the registered T2 rule shrinks a mean of points-for and points-allowed toward a league level, and that mean sits near the league level by construction. Repairing it would be a new estimator, which the registration forbids after the fact.

Would closing the one blank row change the verdict?

The one blank row is not a mystery any more, and this workspace still does not fill it. The frozen corpus records WAS at DAL (week 2) as “Hidden Score”. On 2026-11-16 this session read the official game centre and it publishes WAS 20 – DAL 37, total 57, with quarter splits that sum to the published totals. The repository's own 2026-11-15 capture already agreed. Filling the cell here would be a retrospective edit made after seeing both estimators' errors, which the pre-registration forbids — so the row stays excluded and the correction is requested of the corpus owner (question 2T-Q6).
