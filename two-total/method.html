The scored quantity, and what is deliberately not scored

Primary statistic: the paired difference in mean absolute error on the same games. Companion: root mean squared error, so a mean-absolute-error result cannot be reported alone. Win rate of "which estimator was closer" is printed as a descriptive count and is not treated as a skill measure. No price, ROI, CLV or staking number is computed anywhere in this workspace.
