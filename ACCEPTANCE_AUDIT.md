## Irregularities surfaced for review

1. **Conflicting injury counts are different dated artifacts, not one timeless value.** The repository includes the 254-row 2026-11-12 06:32Z committed snapshot, a separate 279-row read at 2026-11-12 22:34Z, and the 347-row 2026-11-16 Week 10 capture. The root README still describes the 279-row page as live without clearly calling it a historical observation. Use the latest dated capture for current state and preserve older captures as history; do not overwrite one with another.
2. **The source classes differ across workspaces.** The official-source price policy forbids price-dependent backtests without licensed rights, while the origination research artifacts include community-market inputs. Keep those explicitly separate. This audit does not treat community prices as official or licensed.
3. **A cross-workspace presentation conflict was corrected.** `origination/leaderboard.html`, `skill-lab/leaderboard.html`, and `official-verify/leaderboard.html` previously displayed retrospective score tables. They now show no score rows and link to `forward-ledger/leaderboard.html`, the prospective-only board. Historical diagnostics remain accessible elsewhere and must not flow back into a leaderboard.
4. **Historical flag values need dates.** The origination flags were qualified in this pass: the old 2026-11-12 snapshot records four no-injury sections, while the later 2026-11-16 Week 10 read records zero and one inactive article is indexed. The older values remain valid observations, not current availability. Other historical pages may still need the same dated treatment.
5. **The snapshot hash is not a raw-source hash for this read.** `injury-archive/data/index.jsonl` records `raw_bytes_retained: false` and reconstructed markup. Hash integrity does not prove byte-exact capture or reuse rights.
6. **No reward pool or operating terms are established.** Funding, jurisdiction, and prize terms are not inferred or announced. They remain decisions requiring an authorized policy and source evidence.
