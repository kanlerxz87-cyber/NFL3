THE MATCHUP DESK

Depth of replacement

Not whether a starter is replaced, but which published string replaces him — and how far down the chart the club had to go.

The requirement this answers. "Track the depth of the replacement, not just the fact of a replacement." For every player the league expects not to play, this page publishes the string of the next man the club lists behind him, how many strings were skipped to reach him, and whether that man is himself carrying a designation. A replacement who is Questionable is flagged, not counted as clean.
