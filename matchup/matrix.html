THE MATCHUP DESK

Offence vs defence matchup matrix

Which published string of the defence each offensive string actually faces, for every group of every Week 10 game.

What a cell means. For one game and one offensive group, the page shows the string of the shallowest offensive player the club still publishes as available, the string of the shallowest defender in the opposing group, and the difference. A positive difference means the defence is reaching deeper into its chart than the offence is — an offensive advantage on paper, and nothing more: no official source publishes what any of this is worth in points.
