Sources

Every page this workspace read, what was taken from it, and what was deliberately not used.

Authority boundary. Every value on these pages comes from nfl.com or from a club site reached through the official NFL team index. A capture hash proves that bytes did not change, not that the publisher grants reuse rights or that the page will still say the same thing tomorrow. No page here is a point-in-time archive; each is a live document that was read on the date shown.
