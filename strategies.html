RESEARCH

Strategy lab

Candidate strategies for singles, parlays and player-prop combinations, the leaderboard they would enter, and the gates every entry must pass.

Leaderboard rule. An entry becomes eligible only after all ten gates pass. The gates require an official source URL, a raw hash and an official outcome confirmation for every record, so the leaderboard is empty by construction until a licensed price feed and an outcome source exist.
