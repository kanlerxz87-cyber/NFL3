02 / VALIDATION

Gates before a leaderboard

Proposed protocol; thresholds must be fixed before evaluation.

GateRequirementFailure conditionSource / context
1 · ProvenanceRecord source, availability time, retrieval time, rights and version for every input.Missing, stale, retrospective or unlicensed inputs.Review source ↗
2 · LeakageFreeze features at prediction time; split chronologically and group related bets by game.Future information or related outcomes cross folds.Review source ↗
3 · BaselinesCompare against a frozen simple baseline and a timestamped market benchmark where licensed.No comparable benchmark or reproducibility.Review source ↗
4 · CalibrationEvaluate held-out log loss, Brier score and calibration by market with uncertainty intervals.Predeclared calibration or sample requirements not met.Review source ↗
5 · EconomicsBacktest fixed stakes with fees, spreads, pushes, voids and available prices.Results cannot be settled or independently reproduced.Review source ↗
6 · RobustnessUse multiple seasons and game-clustered intervals; correct for multiple strategy searches.Intervals too wide or evidence disappears in untouched holdout.Review source ↗
7 · ShadowFreeze rules and score prospective predictions before considering real-money use.No independent prospective record.Review source ↗
