Correlation plan

What will be estimated

For binary leg outcomes, report empirical pairwise association (with uncertainty) and the observed joint event probability, stratified only where preplanned sample supports it. Compare to the independence product as a baseline. For the full parlay, use joint outcome counts or a validated joint probability model; pairwise correlation alone does not determine a multi-leg joint distribution.

No coefficient is available: —. A correlation coefficient without specified outcomes, matching timestamp, market lines and observations would be invented.

Required safeguards

  • Use only legs with immutable event and line IDs and documented settlement.
  • Pair legs observed at matching pre-event cutoffs; separate same-game and cross-game combinations.
  • Prevent repeated observations of the same game from masquerading as independent evidence.
  • Report uncertainty and sample counts; do not present a stable estimate below a preapproved coverage threshold.
