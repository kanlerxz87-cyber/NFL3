What survived re-checking, and what did not

The published site makes specific factual claims. This pass opened each cited source again on 2026-11-12 and compared the claim with what the page shows now. 15 of 31 claims still hold; 13 are contradicted and 3 are real gaps where the site is silent about something the official source publishes.

The headline problem is not a bad source — it is a silent one. The published Week 10 injury capture contains 254 rows and declares Seattle and Cincinnati injury-free. The live official report contains 279 Seahawks-and-Bengals-inclusive rows and declares only the Chargers and Ravens injury-free. Nothing on the page warned that the two disagree, because a failed parse and a genuinely empty section render identically.

What this means for originating lines

1 · The injury feed is not yet trustworthy

Availability is the feature the whole personnel thesis rests on. Until a capture can prove it saw every club section, any lineup-driven signal inherits a 25-row hole and two phantom injury-free teams.

See the blocking questions →

2 · Depth is a snapshot, not a state

Baltimore's chart still lists a quarterback who went on injured reserve. Depth pages lag roster reality, and the NFL publishes the reconciliation source — the transactions log — which this site does not capture.

See the fix queue →

3 · Markets remain the hard blocker

No line can be scored without permissioned historical prices and a settlement contract. That is confirmed again, not changed: the NFL serves partner-bookmaker prices, not an archive.
