00 / THE SHORT VERSION

What this workspace can and cannot say today

What is established

  • The results feed reproduces nfl.com's official standings for all 32 clubs on every field — wins, losses, ties, points for, points against — over 137 played games.
  • The official depth pages describe 1,386 slots across 30 clubs, and 149 rank-1 slots name more than one player. A single "starter" per slot would be a fabrication.
  • The injury report is captured with a hash and a clock time; every published availability row links to it.
  • Every strategy is pre-registered with a content hash and scored with a proper score, an interval and a control arm.

What is not

  • Thin price history. Two captures exist and 7 of 78 pre-kickoff cells moved between them. Weeks 1-9 kicked off before the first capture, so their numbers are post-hoc and cannot support a closing-line claim.
  • No validated strategy. Every scored rule has a Brier-skill interval that includes zero or sits below it. Two have already failed their own kill rule.
  • No measured injury effect. The personnel ablation's holdout interval is -0.185 to 0.102 points of MAE — it cannot distinguish an effect from noise.
  • No replacement certainty. No official source names a replacement, so that column stays blank.

02 / LUCK

Removing luck from the equation

A win rate cannot separate skill from variance at this sample size. The panel below shows how much sample a real edge needs, and what each scored strategy's interval actually covers.

A 10% edge would need about 153 graded legs; a 2% edge would need almost 4,000. This is why a 12-8 run is not evidence.
