Verdict.
  • One official injury snapshot (2026-11-12). A player hurt after that observation is not in this ablation, so the personnel term under-counts late-week changes.
  • Snap share measures a role, not the value of a player. Replacing a high-share left tackle with another high-share player would show no effect by construction.
  • 137 played games cannot resolve a 1-2 point effect on a 13.5 point margin sd. The interval is the result, not the point estimate.
  • λ is not fitted. The full grid is published so a reader can see that no λ rescues the signal in this sample.
