Suggestions

Research · not betting advice
Read this first. This workspace originates its own numbers and grades them against a reconciled official results feed. It contains community-sourced price snapshots but no licensed price source, so no price-dependent edge, closing-line value or validated strategy is eligible. The prospective leaderboard remains empty until its frozen gates clear. Prices are labelled COMMUNITY; standings and injury reports are labelled OFFICIAL; anything that could not be confirmed is blank and sits on Flagged for review. Nothing here is betting advice.

9 suggestions, ranked by what they unblock

This page is a review surface, not a build queue. Pick a path; nothing here changes a file until it is chosen. Each suggestion names what it unblocks, its cost, its risk and the decision it is waiting on.

Ranked by (blocked requirements unblocked) then by cost. A suggestion with no blocked requirement behind it is an improvement, not a priority.

1Run the capture schedule that already exists
The capture script is written. Pointing a scheduled job at the official report on Wed/Thu/Fri and at kickoff-minus-60 costs almost nothing and creates the series every availability feature needs.		 R12, R21, R41, R42, R46, R52, G05Low (a scheduled job + a monitor) A capture that overwrites rather than appends destroys the series. The store must stay immutable per observation.Approve enabling the scheduled job. NFL injury report (OFFICIAL) ↗
2Buy or licence one historical price archive
One licensed archive with a stated open/close timestamp would convert most of this site's blanks into numbers. Nothing else on this list unlocks as much.		 R01, R18, R29, R50, R51, G05High (licence cost + legal review) A cheap archive with no timestamp flag would be worse than none: it cannot be labelled open vs close, so every CLV claim stays invalid.Approve a budget and a vendor shortlist. 3Capture official inactive lists per game
The single biggest evidence gap in the availability work. Without inactive lists a listed player cannot be confirmed absent, so a replacement is suspected and never established.		 R25, R38, R45, R06, R35Medium (a source + a parser + a daily schedule) An inactive list captured after the game is not point-in-time and cannot be used for a backtest.Approve the source and the capture window. 4Make the demotion rule binding on the owner, not just the build
Two rules have already failed their kill condition. A leaderboard that demotes but never acts is a dashboard, not a governance system.		 R08, R17, G10Low Demoting aggressively can bury a rule that needed one more week. The kill condition is therefore pre-registered, not discretionary.Approve that a demoted rule needs a new hash to return. NFL standings (OFFICIAL); gate artifact: origination/data/gates.json ↗
5Fund the sample, not the model
Every scored rule is within noise. More modelling effort on 137 games will produce a better-looking number, not a better number. The cheapest path to a decision is more graded legs at fixed, pre-registered rules.		 R32, R50, R60Low in money, high in patience Re-using this corpus with new rules inflates the multiplicity problem; new legs must be genuinely out of sample.Approve a fixed evaluation window and no rule changes inside it.
