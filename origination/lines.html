Read this first. This workspace originates its own numbers and grades them against a reconciled official results feed. It contains community-sourced price snapshots but no licensed price source, so no price-dependent edge, closing-line value or validated strategy is eligible. The prospective leaderboard remains empty until its frozen gates clear. Prices are labelled COMMUNITY; standings and injury reports are labelled OFFICIAL; anything that could not be confirmed is blank and sits on Flagged for review. Nothing here is betting advice.
