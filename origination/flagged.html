Read this first. This workspace originates its own numbers and grades them against a reconciled official results feed. It holds one community price observation, so no opening-line edge, closing-line value or validated strategy exists yet — and the leaderboard says so on every row. Prices are labelled COMMUNITY; standings and injury reports are labelled OFFICIAL; anything that could not be confirmed is blank and sits on Flagged for review. Nothing here is betting advice.
