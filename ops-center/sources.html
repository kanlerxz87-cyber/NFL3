Authority boundary

An official NFL or club page establishes a published fact for review. A capture hash proves the bytes arrived intact; it does not prove authorship, accuracy, reuse rights or that the page will still say the same thing tomorrow. Community nflverse assets are useful for exploration and are excluded from every official-only gate.
