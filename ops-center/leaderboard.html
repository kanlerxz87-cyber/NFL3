Removing luck: the sample-size arithmetic

These numbers are computed from declared parameters, not measured from NFL data. That is the point: they say what would be required before any claim of edge is credible.

Win-probability test: n = (z_{1-a/2}*sqrt(p0(1-p0)) + z_power*sqrt(p1(1-p1)))^2 / (p1-p0)^2 with alpha=0.05 two-sided and power=0.80. ROI test: n = ((z_a+z_b)*sd/edge)^2 with sd=1.0 per-bet ROI, i.e. the fully conservative case.

Detecting a 2-point edge over break-even at 80% power needs roughly 4,887 graded bets on the win/loss test and 19,623 on the ROI test. A 40-bet hot streak cannot clear it.

WHY PARLAYS ARE NOT WHAT THEY LOOK LIKE

Independence overstates the risk, and understates it, in the wrong places.

On 2,926 community-data games a three-leg same-game parlay hit 14.6% of the time against 12.8% if the legs were independent — a lift of 1.14x. The home-win and favourite-cover legs are near-duplicates whenever the home side is the favourite, which is exactly why a three-leg same-game parlay is not three independent bets.
