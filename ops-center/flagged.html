OF-01CriticalThe official injury page changed 25 rows in sixteen hoursThe 06:32Z capture holds 254 rows across 24 clubs with four 'No Injuries Reported' sections. Re-read at 22:25Z the same day the page held 279 rows: Seattle and Cincinnati had published 19 rows that did not exist in the morning, six more rows were added to four clubs, and four Colts practice statuses moved in both directions. Nothing was removed.A single capture cannot support any availability feature. Questionable to Out transitions and the Thursday to Friday practice progression are only recoverable from a dated series.Store every capture with its timestamp and hash and keep the history. Treat any figure computed from one capture as provisional.nfl.com ↗
OF-02CriticalPatriots depth rows are labelled 'club depth chart' but the page disclaims itselfThe committed capture labels all 60 Patriots depth rows with authority 'club depth chart'. The page itself states: 'The 2026 depth chart is unofficial and is published by the writers of Patriots.com. The depth chart reflects the opinions of the Patriots.com writers and don't necessarily reflect those of the organization.'Any chain built on Patriots rows inherits an opinion, not a club statement. Ben Brown, Carlton Davis III and TreVeyon Henderson are all ruled Out and all three chains come from this page.Re-label those rows as club-published unofficial and exclude them from official-only gates until the source says otherwise.patriots.com ↗

OF-06MediumPublished strings are not always one playerThe Seahawks chart packs Cooper Kupp and Rashid Shaheed into a single first-string wide-receiver cell; the Commanders and Patriots publish two and three separate rows at the same position, so more than one player carries the same published rank.'First string' and 'second string' are ambiguous at those positions. OF-07MediumStatus is a category, not a probabilityThe official policy defines Did Not Participate, Limited (less than 100 percent of normal repetitions) and Full (100 percent). Game status: Questionable means uncertain, Doubtful means unlikely, Out means will not play. The policy states a player certain to play is not listed in the Game Status Report at all.A Questionable designation carries no calibrated playing probability. Any model that converts these labels into likelihoods is inventing numbers.Use status as an ordinal feature only, and calibrate probabilities against graded outcomes once a licensed result history exists.nfl.com ↗
OF-09MediumInherited strategy text carries figures computed on community dataSome strategy rows merged from other workspaces quote correlations, p-values and matchup constants in their thesis or risk text. Those numbers were computed on nflverse community assets, not on official NFL data, but the row they sit in carries an official source link, which makes them look better sourced than they are.A reader can mistake a community-data figure for an official-source finding.Each row now carries a Basis marker: 'carries computed figures' or 'hypothesis only'. Treat any marked row as exploratory until it is recomputed on a licensed source.github.com ↗
