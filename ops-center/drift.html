Game-status designations

Six players carry a game status. None changed between the two observations. The official policy (OS-02) defines Out as “the player will not play”, Doubtful as “unlikely the player will participate” and Questionable as “uncertain as to whether the player will play”, and states that a player certain to play is not listed in the Game Status Report at all.

Why this decides the design

A single daily capture is not a usable availability history. Questionable to Out transitions, and the Thursday to Friday practice-status changes that precede them, are only recoverable from a series of dated captures. Any feature built on one snapshot silently carries hindsight.
